Create one complete independent illustration of the initial Living Room in Zork I, for the West of House illustrated adventure. This is an unbound concept: do not draw labels, state numbers or any game UI.

Use the references for material and artistic continuity, not their furnishings or extra windows.

Canonical initial scene: A large oriental rug lies in the center of the room, entirely concealing the trapdoor beneath it; show no trapdoor, opening, seam, handle or outline through the rug.
